Property market brief

Week of 16 Nov 2026 · for Ger & Maura · €1.2m+ sales in Monkstown, Blackrock/Booterstown, Dún Laoghaire/Glenageary, Glasthule, Sandycove, Dalkey, Killiney, Foxrock, Mount Merrion, Sandymount, Ballsbridge and Donnybrook

Trend: Bidding has picked up this autumn. €1.2m+ homes in your areas sold for a median 6.9% over asking in the last 12 weeks, up from 3.9% over the previous 40 weeks. The €2m+ band went from selling at asking (0%) to about 5% over. Price levels are flat year on year (median about €1.59m), with €/m² edging up about 3%. So sold-vs-asking is rising and price levels are flat to slightly rising. Confidence is moderate overall and low for €2m+ (only about 20 recent sales with a known asking price).

Reading it:

  • Sold vs asking is rising (moderate confidence). All €1.2m+: median +6.9% and 77% over asking in the last 12 weeks (79 sales with a known asking price), against +3.9% and 65% in the previous 40 weeks (174 sales). The last 4 weeks look similar (+6.1%, 18 sales).
  • Doer-upper band, €1.2m–€1.99m: rising, moderate confidence. Median +7.5% over asking in the last 12 weeks, against +4.8% before. €/m² is about flat (€8,290 vs €8,200). Expect to pay about 5–10% over the asking price for anything decent, and well over 20% for under-priced homes (for example 23 Anglesea Park at +24% and 52 St John’s at +15%).
  • Turnkey band, €2m+: firming, low confidence. Median +5.5% over asking in the last 12 weeks (20 sales), against 0% before (41 sales). The median price is flat at about €2.46m–€2.50m. Over-priced launches still get cut hard first (Baggot Rath −29% from launch, Windy Nook −27%), but homes priced right are now getting bids.
  • Volume is flat to slightly up. 475 sales in the last 52 weeks, against 444 the year before. The last 12 weeks show 106 so far, against 118 in the same weeks of 2025, but that gap is mostly the registration lag.
  • Price level is flat. The 52-week median was €1.59m, against €1.593m the year before. The €/m² matched to floor areas was €8,450 over 52 weeks and €8,645 over the last 12 weeks, about 3% above the previous 40 weeks.
