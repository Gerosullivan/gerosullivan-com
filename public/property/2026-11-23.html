Property market brief

Week of 23 Nov 2026 · for Ger & Maura · €1.2m+ sales in Monkstown, Blackrock/Booterstown, Dún Laoghaire/Glenageary, Glasthule, Sandycove, Dalkey, Killiney, Foxrock, Mount Merrion, Sandymount, Ballsbridge and Donnybrook

Trend: Bidding is holding at a high level, not accelerating. In the last 12 weeks, €1.2m+ homes in your areas sold for a median 6.5% over asking, and 77% sold over. That’s still well above the 3.9% median of the previous 40 weeks, but it hasn’t moved since last week’s 6.9%, and the last 4 weeks show 5.6%. Doer-upper band (€1.2–1.99m): flat but strong, at a median of +6.7%, with under-priced original houses still going 20–27% over. Turnkey €2m+: firmer than earlier in the year, at +5.6% over the last 12 weeks against 0% before, but the last 4 weeks are mixed. Price levels are flat year on year (median about €1.6m), and €/m² is up about 4%. Confidence is moderate overall and low for €2m+.

Reading it:

  • Sold vs asking: flat at a high level (moderate confidence). All €1.2m+: median +6.5% and 77% over asking in the last 12 weeks (83 sales with a known asking price), against +3.9% and 65% in the previous 40 weeks (183 sales). The rise came over the summer and peaked with September sales (+9.0%). October sales came in at +4.6% and the last 4 weeks at +5.6%, so it has levelled off rather than kept climbing. Last week’s 12-week figure was +6.9%.
  • Doer-upper band, €1.2m–€1.99m: flat, moderate confidence. Median +6.7% over asking in the last 12 weeks (60 sales), against +8.0% in June–August and +5.75% in the 40 weeks before. €/m² is flat at about €8,290 (against €8,200). Anything under-priced and original still goes 20%+ over asking (this week: Frascati Park, Richmond Avenue and Crosthwaite Park East). Budget for asking plus 15–25% on a well-located €1.0–1.5m-asking period house.
  • Turnkey band, €2m+: rising, low confidence. Median +5.6% over asking in the last 12 weeks (23 sales), against 0% over the previous 40 weeks (43 sales). The last 4 weeks are mixed, with only 4 sales with a known asking price: Malindi Lodge +8.9%, Proteus +3.3%, Annao Santo −2.7% and Windy Nook −12.9%. The median price is flat at €2.5m, while the median €/m² for €2m+ sales rose to about €10,000 over 12 weeks (21 sales with known sizes), against €9,270 before.
  • Volume is up. 477 sales in the last 52 weeks, against 442 the year before (+8%). The last 12 weeks show 109 so far, against 122 in the same weeks of 2025, which is registration lag.
  • Price level is flat; €/m² is edging up. The 52-week median was €1.598m, against €1.593m the year before. The €/m² matched to floor areas was €8,440 over 52 weeks and €8,720 over the last 12 weeks, about 4% above the previous 40 weeks. That fits the CSO’s figure of Dublin +3.9% year on year (see C).
